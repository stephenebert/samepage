1. Open a fresh shared room with Alice in English and Bob in Spanish in separate tabs. Show the same agreed document.

3. In **The gathering**, preserve the original event sentence and add a registration deadline: Alice says 4 November at 18:00; Bob says 6 November at 18:00. The unchanged **Registration closes** section still says 5 November at 18:00. Claim the two proposals.
4. Show the concrete conflict: at noon on 5 November, one proposal says registration has closed; the other says it has not. Let both editors choose the Nov 4 proposal.
